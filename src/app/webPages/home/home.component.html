Self-Service Dashboard

Welcome to the Secure Mail Platform dashboard. This interface provides a comprehensive snapshot of your communication activity. At a glance, you can view the total number of emails sent, drafts saved, and any scheduled messages for future delivery. The platform also tracks your engagement with both candidates and vendors, giving you clear insights into your outreach operations.

The Sent Mails graph visualizes your email activity over the past seven days, showing trends and helping you identify peak performance days. This helps you plan and optimize future campaigns more effectively. Additionally, the Email Type Distribution pie chart provides a breakdown of the categories your emails fall into, allowing for better understanding and management of your communication strategy.

Use this dashboard to monitor progress, track usage, and maintain control over all mail-related operations within your organization.
