Grievance Redressal Policy
Purpose

This policy ensures a transparent, structured process for handling grievances raised by users.

Resolution Timelines
  • Acknowledgement within 48 hours
  • Resolution within 7 working days (based on complexity)
Priority Levels
  • Low: Minor issue, does not affect workflow
  • High: Major disruption, needs immediate action
  • Critical: Impacts multiple users or business continuity
Escalation

If unresolved within the timeframe, users can escalate to support@mailcept.com.
